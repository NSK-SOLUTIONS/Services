About NSK Solutions

One roof, six trades,
built for Dharmapuri & Salem.

NSK Solutions is based on the Salem Bypass in Adhiyaman Kottai, Dharmapuri, working directly with homes, offices and builders across the region. Instead of chasing separate vendors for windows, interiors, false ceilings and everyday supplies, our customers make one call — and every job, from a single UPVC window to a full office fit-out, is measured, fabricated and installed by the same team.

That range is deliberate. It means the partition matches the false ceiling, the glass work matches the aluminium, and someone is always accountable for the finish — right through to the appliances and packing material that furnish the space afterwards.
